# 寒冬降临（Winter Falls）

[English](#english) | [中文](#中文)

---

# **English**

## Description

Turn the entire Overworld into an eternal winter.
Select the **"Winter Falls"** world type when creating a new world to enable all winter mechanics.

## Features

* It snows forever in the Overworld — the vanilla weather cycle is completely disabled;
* Snow accumulates naturally and gets thicker over time;
* All exposed water surfaces freeze into ice automatically;
* Dense winter fog during nighttime and snowy weather;
* No more sunrise/sunset colors — the sky takes on a pale wintery gray.

* Mob mechanics
  * Snow Golems: Spawn with a low chance in any biome; no longer die from weather or temperature;
  * Strays: Spawn with a chance in any biome; naturally spawned Skeletons have a chance to convert into Strays;
  * Polar Bears: Spawn with a low chance in any biome, usually as a family group (adults + cubs);
  * Foxes: Spawn with a low chance in any biome, always white Arctic foxes; low chance to spawn as a kit; adult foxes have a 15% chance to hold items in their mouth, kits have 35%;
  * Rabbits: Naturally spawned rabbits are always white;

## Configuration

* Whether to override vanilla precipitation (force snow everywhere)
* Whether to disable the vanilla weather cycle
* Snow accumulation check interval and probability
* Maximum snow layer thickness
* Spawn probability and count for all featured mobs

# **中文**

## 简介

将整个主世界变成永恒的寒冬。
创建世界时选择 **"寒冬降临"** 世界类型即可启用所有冬天机制。

## 特性

* 主世界永远下雪，原版天气循环被禁用；
* 进入世界即铺满雪层，雪会自然增厚；
* 所有露天水面自动冻结成冰；
* 夜晚和雪天有浓浓的冬日雾气；
* 去除日出日落色彩，天空呈冬日灰白。

* 生物机制
  * 雪傀儡：任何群系都有低概率生成；不会再被天气和温度影响而死亡；
  * 流浪者：任何群系都有概率生成；自然生成的骷髅有概率转换为流浪者；
  * 北极熊：任何群系中北极熊都有低概率的生成，且会拖家带口的生成；
  * 狐狸：任何群系中都有低概率生成，且必定是白色极地狐；生成时有低概率是小狐狸；大狐狸有15%概率叼物品，小狐狸则有35%概率；
  * 兔子：自然生成的兔子必定是白色的；
  * 狼：自然生成的狼必定是白色；

## 配置

* 是否覆盖原版降水（强制下雪）
* 是否禁用原版天气循环
* 积雪检查间隔与概率
* 最大雪层厚度
* 特色生物的生成概率和数量
